What this is

Three small daily YouTube channels, and the pages that go with them: a falling-notes piano tutorial with the free score and MIDI for every piece, a classic audiobook chapter with the full text to read along, and a long-form history episode with its complete transcript and sources. Everything here is public domain or openly licensed.
